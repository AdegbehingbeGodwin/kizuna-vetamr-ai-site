Methods and evidence

Reference data that can be reviewed

Extract veterinary antibiotic product details, explore reference records, and review package-to-ingredient calculations in one workspace.

The catalog is a curated demonstration, with incomplete record-level source evidence. The frequency charts describe catalog records, not global sales or national consumption. Parser scores reflect completeness, not calibrated confidence.

Extraction is evaluated on two illustrative English development fixtures. This does not establish independent or multilingual accuracy. Review the committed evaluation output for every field prediction and mismatch.

Biomass and dose calculations are exploratory. ANIMUSE-style exports, classifications, and regulatory screening rules require specialist validation. A SHA-256 checksum does not prove source authenticity.
